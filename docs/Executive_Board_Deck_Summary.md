# Executive Board Deck Narrative (5-Slide C-Suite Summary)
**Project:** Commercial Profitability & Discount Leakage Strategy  
**Presented by:** Shivastha Rewaskar (Lead Commercial Analytics)  
**Audience:** Chief Financial Officer (CFO), Chief Revenue Officer (CRO), Board of Directors  

---

### Slide 1: The Topline Illusion (Revenue Growth vs. Margin Erosion)
* **The Reality:** While Gross Pipeline surged past **$110.37M**, unmonitored commercial concessions resulted in **$14.96M in discretionary discounts granted** (average effective discount: 13.55%).
* **The Strategic Risk:** Topline growth masks systemic profitability decay in high-growth sales territories.

---

### Slide 2: The "Discount Cliff" & 540 Underwater Deals
* **The Finding:** Analysis uncovered that **540 closed enterprise deals yielded negative net profit**, draining **$151,781.40** directly from operating cash flow.
* **The Inflection Point:** 
  - Discounts between **0% and 20%** produced **zero loss-making transactions**.
  - Discounts exceeding **20%** triggered catastrophic margin collapse, where sales commissions were paid on revenue that actively lost money for the firm.

---

### Slide 3: Cross-Category Cannibalization (Hardware Sold at Software Discounts)
* **The Root Cause:** Sales representatives applied Software-level discounting rules (30%+) to low-margin Hardware lines (IoT Sensor Hubs, Server Racks with unit margins < 22%).
* **Territory Accountability:** NA-West and APAC-South accounted for **68.2% of all negative-margin deals**, driven by quota structures indexed on gross revenue rather than gross profit.

---

### Slide 4: Simulation Model: What Happens If We Cap Discounts at 18%?
* **Executive What-If Model:**
  - Introducing an automated discount approval hard-cap at **18%** across low-margin categories.
  - **Instant EBITDA Recovery:** **+$129,014.19** directly returned to net operating income.
  - **Zero Customer Attrition Risk:** Historical deal velocity shows 94% of deals within 15–20% close without requiring escalation to 30%+.

---

### Slide 5: Strategic Action Plan & Governance Overhaul
1. **Commission Realignment (Day 1):** Shift sales representative commission plans from **Gross Revenue Booking** to **Gross Margin Contribution ($)**.
2. **CPQ System Hard-Stops (Day 30):** Implement automated workflow gates in Salesforce/HubSpot CPQ preventing discounts > 18% without VP Finance digital sign-off.
3. **Product-Category Specific Discount Matrix:** Establish differentiated discount ceilings: Software (max 25%), Services (max 15%), Hardware (max 8%).
